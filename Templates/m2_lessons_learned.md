# Template: M2 Lessons Learned

Output format — Google Doc, one single living document at
`50_QA_Department_Standards\m2_lessons_learned`. Personal, retrospective,
append-only log of your own management-activity lessons — what you'd do
differently, what worked and is worth repeating. Distinct from
`qa_department_standards` (department-wide, current-state, what's
expected of you) and from `pm_case_library` (cross-project situation
patterns that are reusable independent of who was managing). This
document is specifically about *your own* practice.

Not a place to record a verdict about another named person's competence
or reliability — that's `individual_risk`/`individual_metrics`. A lesson
here is about a decision or approach *you* took as M2, and what you
learned from how it played out.

Append-only, chronological (newest at the end, same append direction as
other append-only logs in this repo) — never rewritten in place, since
the point is a genuine trail of how your own approach has evolved over
time. If a later entry supersedes an earlier one's takeaway, say so in
the new entry rather than editing the old one.

Each entry:

> **[YYYY-MM-DD, Project if relevant]** What I did / the situation. What
> I'd do differently next time, or what worked and why it's worth
> repeating. Keep it concrete enough to act on next time a similar
> situation comes up — not a vague "communicate better."
